Welcome to the Hunt Lab at the University of Chicago!
Our work investigates how multiphase fluids, interfaces, solids, and solutes interact. We use a combination of custom experiments, modelling, and computation to study phenomena that arise from these complex interactions, often motivated by oceanic and atmospheric systems.

We are accepting undergrads, graduate students, and postdocs, so please reach out if you are interested in working with us!
